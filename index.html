Privacy Policy for HeroRush

Last updated 20 August 2026 · Applies to HeroRush (com.mydatawave.herorush) on Android and iOS

HeroRush does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics service attached. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains what that means in practice, and what the game does keep on your own device.

Information we collect

None. We operate no servers that receive data from the game, and the game contains no third-party advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or analytics software.

Specifically, HeroRush does not collect:

  • your name, email address, or any contact details
  • accounts or login credentials — the game has none
  • advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or IP-based tracking
  • location data
  • contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera access
  • payment information

What the game stores on your device

HeroRush saves your progress locally, on your own phone or tablet, so the game remembers where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us.

What is saved Why
Selected hero, equipped elements, element and skill levels To restore your loadout between sessions
Coins, and lifetime coins earned and spent To track your in-game currency
Personal records, such as best run per hero To show your own best results
Settings, such as sound preferences To keep your chosen options
Whether the tutorial has been completed, and your consent choice So you are not asked twice

All of it is game state. None of it identifies you.

To erase it, uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage in your device settings. Deleting the app deletes this data. Because it is stored only on your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request you need to send us.

Network access

The Android version declares the standard internet permission, which is required by the framework HeroRush is built with. The game loads its graphics and sounds from files included in the app itself, not from the network, and does not use this permission to send your information anywhere.

Children

HeroRush does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since the game collects no personal data at all, there is nothing about a child user for us to receive, store, or disclose.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. HeroRush includes no advertising networks, no social media integrations, and no analytics providers.

The app stores themselves — Google Play and the Apple App Store — collect their own information when you download or purchase an app, under their own privacy policies. That collection is theirs, not ours, and we receive only the aggregate statistics those stores show every developer, which do not identify individual players.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none. If you would like confirmation of that in writing, or have any question about this policy, contact us using the address below and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of HeroRush ever adds analytics, advertising, or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the store listing's data disclosures will be updated to match. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
